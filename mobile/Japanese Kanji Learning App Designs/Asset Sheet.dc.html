Rules for drawing more
Shape
Everything fits a 100×100 box. Squircle tiles at rx 27, circles at r 46. No sharp corners anywhere — the smallest radius in the set is 6.5.
Weight
Strokes are 9–11 at this scale, round caps and joins, no exceptions. Thin lines vanish at 22px and break the family.
Colour
One saturated fill plus white marks. Only the empty states may use several colours, and only as confetti dots.
Restraint
Four elements maximum inside a tile. If it needs more to read, it wants to be an illustration, not an icon.
